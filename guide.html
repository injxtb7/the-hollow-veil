The Hollow Veil

A short guide for playing on a phone. Light on spoilers.

Controls (touch)

  • The left joystick moves you. Walk into a road-end or tap USE at a wooden signpost to leave an area.
  • USE talks to people, opens chests, picks herbs, digs, and takes an exit.
  • ATK attacks. DODGE steps you aside. Tap a skill tile under the HUD (or SKILL for the first one).
  • PACK is your inventory (tap items to use or equip). MENU pauses: Journal, Skills, Save, Settings, and this Guide.
  • In talk scenes, tap a choice. In a shop, tap buy, sell, or Close (kept above the joystick).

Exits

Every road out has a small wooden sign with the destination and an arrow. Walk onto it, or stand close and tap USE. A prompt like “Walk on or tap USE: Crooked River” appears when you are near. The Next: line on the HUD tells you where to go.

Lantern Bearer

You chose Lantern Bearer: consecrated light against the haint-dark. Keep the lantern in mind in caves and at night. Your staff and lantern are how you are found in the dark. Spend skill points from the pause Skills screen.

The main road (from Yarrow)

  1. Talk to Granny Ila in Yarrow Crossing (look for !).
  2. Walk the scarred marks:
    • Widow’s Bluff — Head east from Yarrow Crossing: Crooked River > Widow's Bluff.
    • Devil’s Promenade — Head east from Yarrow Crossing: Crooked River > Ferry Crossing > Devil's Promenade.
    • Pine Churchyard — Head south from Yarrow Crossing: Pine Churchyard.
  3. Come back and choose a road: Ila (mend the marks), Preacher Cald in Yarrow (purge), or Jonas Peck — Head north from Yarrow Crossing: Post Oak Ridge > High Ledge > Bald Knob.
  4. When you are ready to go under: Head north from Yarrow Crossing: Post Oak Ridge > High Ledge > Harthall Ruins > The Spanish Mine > The Under-Karst. Face the Under-Stone there.

The banjo duel (optional)

  1. Talk to Josie Fiddler in Yarrow, just south of the well (tile 27, 19).
  2. Head north from Yarrow Crossing: Post Oak Ridge > Howler Run > The Crossroads. On the west side (left, x under 16), tap USE to dig up the banjo. No shovel needed.
  3. The Man in the Dark Hat appears at night only (the day is a 12-minute cycle), in the middle of The Crossroads. Talk to him to start the duel. Tap the lane buttons and ROLL — you do not need a keyboard.

Tips

  • Day and night share a 12-minute cycle. Some people and dangers only come out after dark.
  • Heal with packed herbs and food from PACK. If you fall (not Iron Lantern), you wake on Ila’s porch.
  • The Next line and Journal stay current as you go. If you feel lost, open Menu → Journal or this Guide.
